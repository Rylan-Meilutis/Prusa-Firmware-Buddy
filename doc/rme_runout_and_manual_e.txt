INDX serial runout and LCD E jogging — November 4, 2026

Physical side-sensor runout no longer depends on serial collision replay being
armed. Serial Printing with crash state IDLE is valid; actual crash/recovery
states remain excluded. INDX rechecks a confirmed NoFilament level after event
locks/toolchanges clear, rather than losing removal edges while guarded. It
checks only the selected tool, only in stable Printing, outside toolchanges,
and retains the one-M600 latch. Disabled/disconnected/unknown are not reported
as confirmed empty. The latch is reset at new-print initialization.

Pressure-based runout still requires an accepted loadcell reference. A fallback
PA value is not a measurement and cannot establish a safe pressure threshold.
When protection was requested but the selected tool lacks that reference,
sustained executed feed (>3 seconds AND >3 mm) raises reference_unavailable and
pauses with an explicit calibration message instead of silently running without
protection or inventing a runout. Purge/maintenance exclusions remain effective.
The user may recalibrate or explicitly disable the loadcell policy; independent
side-sensor protection is unchanged. M591 now also reports reference readiness.

LCD G123 jogging previously exited before its first segment when another
command was queued. It now plans its first segment, ignores a narrow set of
queued read-only display/temperature/position/progress commands, and yields to
other controls or a replacement jog. This avoids stopping an E jog after a
single tiny segment due to M117 traffic. No-tool, cold-extrusion, homing,
travel-limit and dock keep-out protections remain in effect.

Native tests execute the production pressure monitor, sensor dispatcher and
jog loop. They cover empty tool eight with replay disabled, missed removal
edges, locks/toolchange/recovery suppression, one-shot injection, disconnected
and disabled sensors, fallback-reference reporting and opt-out, maintenance and
purge exclusions, E-only jogging with queued M117, and replacement-jog yielding.
These tests do not validate the physical loadcell/sensor wiring or establish why
a particular real PLA pressure measurement failed. A terminal PA diagnostic is
required to diagnose that separate report; the supplied excerpt ended mid-run.
