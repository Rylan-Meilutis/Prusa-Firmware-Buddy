Correction
----------

marker stat and receipt reads. Marlin only schedules/polls the job; it does
not wait on USB. Do not discard or reissue active cleanup. The active state
synchronizes access to CleanupGate between Marlin and the worker. No new
thread or watchdog bypass is introduced.

Acquire the shared transfer slot inside the worker before accessing storage.
If another producer wins before execution, leave cleanup pending for retry.
Preserve the armed-candidate guard, authorization receipt, candidate and
metadata deletion ordering, retry policy, and marker retention on errors.
