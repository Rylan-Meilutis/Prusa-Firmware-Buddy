TPU startup purge and pressure-monitor correction — November 2, 2026

The November 2 report placed the warning during the initial purge, before the
first object-print move. Its serial excerpt does not include the actual trigger.
The native lab reproduced an independent ambiguity in the released detector:
an already-pressurized first sample, or residual pressure across travel, was
learned as idle zero and could produce no_pressure_rise after about 12 seconds
at 0.5 mm/s. This is a reproducible failure class, not proof of this print's
physical cause. INDX's side sensors and the RME pressure model are separate.

Production correction:
- Bound a startup zero estimate using the accepted slow/fast pressure reference
  only when the first reading is already at the calibrated loaded plateau.
  A lower-force initial reading remains its own local idle baseline.
- Do not overwrite that baseline with the first forward step after travel or
  absorb retained TPU pressure as an unloaded baseline. An extrapolated startup
  estimate is superseded by a later observation below the loaded plateau, so
  steep reference slopes cannot invent residual pressure and hide empty feed.
- Allow 5 percent measurement tolerance at the low executed-velocity boundary;
  retain the existing time AND filament-distance confirmation windows.
- INDX G12 S90 establishes an explicit bin-purge scope after entering the bin.
  Streamed E-only purge moves within that scope are not compared with the
  print-pressure model. S91, executed XY leaving the bin (even without S91),
  print finalization and new-job reset rearm it. Merely queued/planned XY does
  not end a purge. Repeated S90 or tool selection cannot leak a counted hold.
- Internal nozzle-cleaner sequences use a nestable maintenance guard through
  the completion of buffered motion. Physical filament sensors are unaffected.
- Fault logging now identifies the loadcell path with pressure, baseline,
  expected pressure, velocity, forward distance/time and flexible policy. An
  automatic M600 change has a separate source tag; M600 A may also be explicitly
  requested, so that tag alone is not proof of a hardware sensor transition.

The S90/S91 bin scope is INDX-only; the baseline and quantized-feed corrections
apply to maintained firmware lines that actually contain RME pressure monitoring.
No slicer edit is required for the existing S90/purge/S91 start sequence. Custom
scripts that do not enter the bin with S90 do not get this explicit exclusion.
Do not disable side sensors or lower PA reference-quality gates to test it.

Software gates cover the production monitor (not a reimplementation), both load
orientations and offsets, primed startup, retained pressure, bin purge before
any object motion, first object extrusion, true empty feed at several rates
and reference slopes, later collapse, nested maintenance, cancellation and new
jobs. Physical TPU flow and loadcell/thermal behavior still require supervised
testing. Release notes/validation assets identify the exact built revisions.
