RME chamber temperature waiting status
======================================

The actual M191 / M141_no_parser blocking chamber wait now emits a native
chamber_temperature workflow: open at entry, active with current/target in
the message on the first sample and at most once per second, and terminal
closed when ready, skipped on Skip, or cancelled on cancellation/planner drain.
Unknown temperature is labelled unknown, not zero. No misleading percent is
derived from absolute temperatures, particularly during a cooling wait.

M141 without waiting, zero-target commands and unsupported temperature-control
machines do not emit a fake wait. Existing target clamping, 3 C tolerance,
heating/cooling direction selection, indefinite unknown-data waiting, 30-minute
warning, Skip and cancellation behavior are unchanged. Reporting adds no wait
and no heater or fan commands. Existing RME session/event filtering applies.

OctoPrint b165 names and shows this workflow using the existing native workflow
UI. Active updates carry temperature_wait code and do not expire as a legacy
15-second notification. Completion/skip/cancellation close the card normally.
Production-wait simulation covers heating, cooling, already-hot heating-only,
unknown samples, Skip, cancellation, warning/Skip, no-wait and unsupported cases.
No hardware chamber soak test was performed.
