Completion lighting — 2026-11-20

While the Finished result remains unacknowledged, status green follows the
printer's existing activity policy: on while active, off in idle/deep idle,
and on again when activity resumes. The result can remain on screen throughout.
The old completion timer no longer suppresses green on an active Finished
screen. A zero completion-hold setting still disables the green indication.
The bounded timer remains a fallback for completion transitions outside Finished.

Acknowledgement uses the existing post-print hold setting and its door
close-after-open lifecycle, or closing the print-end screen. Merely opening
the door does not acknowledge. No independent filtration door path bypasses
the setting. New prints reset acknowledgement normally.

Filtration keeps its steady progress tint; error/warning indications and
print brightness overrides retain priority. No printer has been flashed or
restarted as part of software validation.
