INDX dock fan: cold park classification
======================================

Timed dock cooldown previously marked every park as hot, even a cold tool
that had never been heated. This started/restarted the fan on cold startup
and tool-change moves when M979 timed cooldown was enabled.

Classify on-head temperature before stop_heating clears it. A finite reading
from 0 C to below 50 C, with target zero and heater PWM zero, proves a cold
park. Such parks do not start or extend timed cooldown. Keep this decision
through park completion/retry; clear it on pickup. A positive target, heater
output or reading >=50 C overrides a previous cold classification.

Unknown/invalid temperature remains potentially hot unless it is a retry of
the same already proven cold park. Park completion without a prior cold
classification also remains conservative. Existing hot cooling intervals are
never shortened or cancelled by a cold park, job completion or cancellation.
Manual dock fan requests, calibration fan guards, thermal protections and
heatbreak fans are unchanged. The threshold is not a cooled-nozzle guarantee.

Native production controller/parser tests cover cold startup, retries, pickup,
hot/unknown/invalid readings, heating requests/output, deadline preservation,
manual mode, cancellation and clock wrap. No hardware fan test is claimed.
