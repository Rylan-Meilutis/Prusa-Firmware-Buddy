Auto PA fast path and verification
=================================

Behavior
--------
- Exact compatible cache hits still return before calibration preparation,
  heating or extrusion. Cache identity and acceptance thresholds are unchanged.
- A compatible measured temperature model supplies a search hint. Within the
  measured temperature range it uses the existing bounded model. Outside that
  range, a hint is allowed only within 15 C of an endpoint; a good linear fit
  supplies at most a +/-0.02 s adjustment. Otherwise use the nearest point.
  A hint NEVER counts as a cache hit or pressure/runout reference.
- Warm verification measures the hint, its +/-0.002 neighbours, then the hint
  independently again. All four captures must pass the configured confidence
  and SNR floors. Both centre captures must beat both neighbours, and their
  costs must agree within max(0.01, 10% of initial centre cost). Clipped boundary
  hints, flat curves, noisy repeats and insufficient signal cannot fast-pass.
- Failed warm verification discards its search observations and starts the
  material-default full search. Thus a rejected hint cannot anchor a bad fit.
- An automatic full search may skip its final refinement once its bracket is
  <=0.006 s with adequate confidence/SNR. Its final three neighbour checks are
  unchanged. Manual/forced runs retain all refinement steps and bypass warm
  verification and cache reuse, providing a baseline comparison path.
- Three consecutive captures lacking sufficient peak signal stop further
  excitation. A good signal resets that counter. Empty/overflowed captures or
  an invalid idle-noise capture fail closed without futile search/retries.
  Normal cleanup and the existing safe fallback remain mandatory; rejected
  measurements never replace a successful flash cache.
- INDX groups temperatures by tool and measures each group lowest-to-highest
  after low-temperature preparation, avoiding needless cooldown waits. The
  original first-layer result is still selected after the plan completes.
  It retains the active calibration target
  and skips redundant pre-probe preparation only after a completed point on
  the same logical tool. Temperature waits include cooling, so a lower point
  cannot accidentally be measured at the preceding higher temperature.
  MMU/other targets retain the original round ordering, unload and sheet-anchor
  lifecycle. INDX skips remaining temperature points after a tool fallback.
- Reuse the extrusion-gap position across candidate captures until cleanup
  actually leaves it. Keep four cycles per capture, five-cycle pellet limit,
  fifteen-second cooling dwell, two native cleaning sweeps and final ejection.
  An additional twelve-millimetre pending filament budget prevents a future
  excitation speed change from overfilling the existing pellet allowance.

Diagnostics
-----------
M976 I L0             retained last-attempt result, failure evidence and usage
M976 I L0 S250        additionally inspect cache eligibility at 250 C

PA_CAL_USAGE includes:
  search: cache, full, warm_verified or warm_then_full (not_started before search)
  elapsed_ms: single-point preparation/search/cleanup time, excluding tool pickup
  measurement_ms: captures including any pellet cleanup inside a capture
  pellet_cleanup_ms: subset spent in periodic/final INDX wipe/cool/eject
  excitation_mm: commanded positive filament feed in calibration bursts ONLY
  measurements, ejections: counts for this point

Do not add measurement_ms and pellet_cleanup_ms: they overlap. Feed accounting
is commanded excitation, not a filament sensor measurement, and excludes native
cleaner/loading motion. These RAM diagnostics retain only the latest attempt per
slot until another attempt or reboot, not the sum of a multi-temperature plan.

Bounds
------
No new heap allocations, dynamic containers or RTOS tasks in the optimizer.
Candidate history retains its previous 26-entry bound (failed warm observations
are discarded before full search); diagnostic history is compile-time
limited to 1024 bytes for all eight slots. Warm verification uses four captures;
full refinement uses at most thirteen before configured confirmations (0..10).
Warm rejection plus full search plus confirmations is bounded at 27 captures.
Cache hit uses zero calibration captures. The temperature bank format is
unchanged because the excitation/scoring and pressure-reference semantics are
unchanged. The existing INDX 44 KiB initial-heap/CCM stack release gate applies.

Validation
----------
New native tests: tests/unit/rme/pa_fast_search_tests.cpp
Executable production-code fixtures: tests/unit/gui/test_m976_fast_search.py

The fixtures compile the actual optimizer/confidence code, burst sequence,
INDX batch runner and batch schedule with deterministic peripheral stubs.
They execute under AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer and cover:
- Correct hint, wrong hint, forced baseline, boundary hints, flat/noisy fits;
- A sweep of 31 optima, faults at each of 17 measurement positions, retry
  budgets 0/1/6/10, and cancellation at positions 0..27;
- Low signal, capture allocation/no-data failure, overflow, invalid idle noise,
  low confidence, invalid costs, timing rejection and repeated-centre drift;
- Cache-only batches with no pickup/excitation, forced bypass, same-tool
  preparation sharing, tool changes and preparation/pickup failures;
- All combinations of 1..8 tools and 1..8 points for INDX and legacy/MMU order;
- Capture release, cleaner contact excluded from capture, failed ejection,
  retained pellet budget, rigid/flexible excitation and planner drain;
- Temperature hint bounds, independent validity of cache hits, and every
  spool/nozzle/quality identity field preventing an incompatible hint;
- Thousands of pellet-budget cycles without exceeding previous limits.

These tests are NOT a whole-printer simulator or a guarantee against crashes.
They do not emulate loadcell mechanics, pellet solidification, simultaneous
STM32 tasks/peripherals, heap fragmentation, thermal timing or long prints.
Hardware comparison of warm verification versus forced full calibration,
including HIPS, is required before claiming accuracy/time/filament savings.
No firmware is flashed or public release changed by these tests.

Reproduction
------------
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests/unit/gui
cmake --build <native-build> --target rme_protocol_tests extrusion_calibration_tests mmu_tests mmu2-modbus_tests
<native-build>/tests/unit/rme/rme_protocol_tests
<native-build>/tests/unit/common/extrusion_calibration/extrusion_calibration_tests
<native-build>/tests/unit/mmu2/mmu_tests
<native-build>/tests/unit/mmu2-modbus/mmu2-modbus_tests

Repeat native executables in a build using -fsanitize=address,undefined and
-fno-omit-frame-pointer. LeakSanitizer is not covered: detect_leaks=0 is needed
in this ptrace-restricted execution environment. Sanitizer errors must halt.
October 21 capture-overflow correction
-------------------------------------
Retained E feedrate/volumetric limits previously stretched four nominal
0.75-second cycles beyond the fixed 1,280-sample buffer. Queue-start waits
were also recorded as measurement data. Reproduced with the production burst,
capture and planner flow-limiter code before this correction.

Excitation speeds/distances now respect existing limits while retaining the
requested plateau durations. Capture waits for executed E movement after each
resume; pre-motion scheduling waits do not consume its buffer. Idle-noise
measurements still retain stationary samples. The capture buffer remains
1,280 samples (no heap increase); producer access is excluded during pointer
release, and allocation/free remain outside the short producer exclusion.
Pressure references are expressed at the nominal monitor/cache velocities,
without increasing measurement confidence or scaling measured noise. Low
signal, allocation failures and true overflow still reject/cache nothing.

Read-only M976 I inspection also bypasses the serial activity wake path, not
only the calibration lighting hold. Numbered/checksummed inspection is covered.
Native policy/capture tests, ASan/UBSan production timing regressions, bounded
search/cleanup tests and firmware builds are release checks; they do not replace
supervised physical printer validation.
