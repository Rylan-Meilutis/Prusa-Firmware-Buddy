RME serial pause/cancel recovery update - 2026-10-19

Failure evidence
---------------
The supplied DUMP_B1 (1).BIN identifies source 84a5c78e15208e64e1aba4ab6afe9d493ea498c5,
6.9.2-RME COREONE_INDX. The stopped serial job had reached Aborted and released
its tool. The ordinary command FIFO was executing N200797 G1 E-6.5, followed
by Z/mode/heater-shutdown commands consistent with a host cancellation macro.
The planner correctly detected an E move without a selected tool, but its
internal assertion crashed firmware for this externally supplied command.
The dump is not proof of the cause of the earlier pause transition stall.

Corrections
-----------
- Keep serial reception and recovery/service dispatch alive while paused,
  pausing, restoring a tool, or at full normal job FIFO capacity.
- Hold queued job commands through every pause/restoration stage. Held host
  moves are not active pause housekeeping; internal recovery injection still
  runs and still counts as busy. Do not wait forever for a deliberately held
  FIFO to drain before parking or restoring the tool.
- Do not overflow the bounded command array. An ordinary numbered line which
  cannot fit is not accepted, restores the prior accepted-line watermark,
  and requests Resend. Excess unnumbered ordinary commands are rejected.
- Report pause_phase=none|pausing|paused|resuming in session snapshots.
  The older coarse PAUSED device state also includes transitional states.
- Reject G0/G1 and G2/G3 extrusion with no selected tool before planning or
  changing current position. Do not weaken the planner's internal assertion,
  permit ordinary G-code to use service-move exemptions, or bypass cold-
  extrusion protection. Legacy branches use their existing raw tool index.
- Companion plugin 0.1.0b135 reconciles missed pause completion without
  replaying M601 or waiting behind held M400/M114 commands. It does not treat
  a pausing snapshot as a completed pause or override a recovery dialog.
- Recovery queries/controls use the existing serialized transport, not a
  second writer. When the host exposes raw-send primitives, these recovery
  frames remain unnumbered and do not consume a blocked job's line number.
- Suppress competing host pause/resume/cancel motion macros on RME printers.
  Firmware owns parking and tool restoration. Unsupported printers retain
  their original macro behavior. Retain reported spaghetti/fault reasons.

Validation and limits
---------------------
Execute tests/unit/gui/test_serial_pause_recovery.py through CTest's
serial_pause_recovery_faults target or directly. The fixtures execute extracted
production FIFO/RX entry points, busy detection, full-FIFO rejection, and G1
handling under AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer. They exercise
1,000 repeats of the exact 6.5 mm retract with no tool, valid selected-tool
retraction and no-tool XYZ-only movement, all pause/restoration stages with a
full FIFO, internal recovery injection, and accepted-line watermark recovery.
Source checks keep service dispatch before capacity rejection and cover the
corresponding arc-command guard. Existing INDX pause parking, resume priming,
Auto PA, font recovery, and MMU checks remain part of the release gates.

Companion plugin tests cover lost pause actions, host Pausing marker waits,
transitional snapshots, stale/terminal jobs, rejected resume retries, original
fault reasons, macro suppression, and recovery line-number independence.

These are host/stub regression checks and ARM build checks, not a complete
physical printer simulator or proof that every firmware path cannot fail.
LeakSanitizer is disabled under the ptrace-restricted test environment;
AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer remain enabled in the new
executable fault fixtures. No printer was flashed, moved, or used for a
spaghetti-pause/cancel hardware test while producing this update.
Supervised hardware validation remains required. Existing Auto PA/font
improvements and the initial-heap release floor remain in place.
